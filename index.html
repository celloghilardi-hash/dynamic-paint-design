How do I request a quote?+

Use the form below to prepare your project details, then send the message to Bruno. Copy the message and send it using your existing contact with Bruno.

What details should I include?+

Tell us your suburb, whether the work is inside or outside, the rooms or surfaces involved and your preferred timing. Photos can help explain the condition and the finish you have in mind.

Can I share photos or colour ideas?+

Yes. After copying your request into your messaging app, attach your photos or inspiration images there.

How is the price worked out?+

The scope, surface condition, preparation, access and finish all affect the price. Bruno can discuss these details with you and arrange a closer look if needed.

When could my project start?+

Include your preferred timing in the request. Bruno will confirm availability after discussing the scope of the work.
